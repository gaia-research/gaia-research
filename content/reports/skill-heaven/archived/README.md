# content/reports/skill-heaven/archived/ — frozen research records

**Never edit anything in this directory.** These are dated research records, not
living documents.

They are excluded from the vocabulary gate
(`scripts/lexicon/check-lexicon.ts`) because they contain vocabulary the line
does not use, and are supposed to.

| Archived | Why |
|---|---|
| `2026-07-24-rfc-68-rrl-1.md` | Research-literature review feeding RFC #68. |
| `2026-07-24-rfc-68-rrl-2.md` | Second RRL pass, same lane. |
| `2026-07-24-rfc-68-synthesis.md` | Synthesis of the two. |

**Why all three at once.** They coined an entire parallel vocabulary — "5-Tier
ACI", `tier` in a sense unrelated to G2's stamps, `firebreak`, "Hell Mode" — and
that vocabulary leaked into live docs without ever passing through the oracle.
After the oracle itself, this was the largest single source of vocabulary drift
on the line.

**What survived them: nothing, deliberately.** The ACI tiering findings are
genuinely interesting and may well be right. If the tier model earns its way
into the product it enters through `founder/lexicon.json` and
`founder/RATIFICATION.md` — not by citation from a research report. That is the
whole point of the consolidation: research proposes, the oracle decides, and a
report is not a side door.

The bibliography (`../rrl-references.md`) stays live — citations do not drift.
